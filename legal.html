Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. We collect only the data necessary to provide a personalized and seamless travel experience. This includes your identity documents for border crossings and your personal preferences for arrival rituals.

Section 03

Cancellation Policy

Our "Peace of Mind" policy ensures flexibility while respecting the preparation of our sanctuary partners. Cancellations are categorized as follows:

  • 30+ Days Prior: 100% Refund or 110% Sanctuary Credit.
  • 14-29 Days Prior: 50% Refund or 75% Sanctuary Credit.
  • Less than 14 Days: No refund, but 25% Sanctuary Credit may be applied in exceptional cases.

Credits are valid for 24 months from the date of issue and can be applied to any destination in our curated collection.
